01A timeline you can actually grab

12 px visual weight inside a 32 px seek target. Played, buffered and unplayed states stay distinct; chapter dividers provide structure. A thumbnail floats above your pointer, never over the controls.

02Volume, on its own axis

Fixed at middle-right, away from transport. A vertical slider increases upward; the percentage and mute button stay together. The narrow backing keeps it legible over bright scenes.

03Everything else, a thumb away

Play and ±10 seconds on the left. Chapters, subtitles, audio, speed, settings and fullscreen on the right. Track pickers open upward. Subtitles sit safely above the entire control zone.

DESIGN PROPOSAL ONLY · All tracks, chapters, buffering and thumbnails are fixtures. Real previews need a separate thumbnail-generation/cache pipeline; no decoding or I/O belongs in UI rendering. Proposed auto-hide: after 2.5 s while playing, never during scrubbing, keyboard focus or an open menu.
