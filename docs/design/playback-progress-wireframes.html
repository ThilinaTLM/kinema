Static review artifact for playback-progress-desktop.md. Nothing here is implemented, qualified on any OS, or wired to a database, mpv, a tray or a media session. Fixture titles, times and artwork are placeholders. Native-menu and window chrome are approximations; each OS draws its own.

Catalog progress visibility: conditional

Progress bar, percent, elapsed text and “Resume · time” appear in cards/detail/episode rows only when the item is unwatched and has a usable checkpoint. A watched item hides them; the checkpoint is retained internally.

Player seek bar: always visible

The embedded player’s seek bar, elapsed/remaining time and OS transport position are accurate regardless of watched status. Hiding catalog progress never hides playback controls.

04Watched checkpoint: Resume prompt in player

Playing a watched title that retains a checkpoint asks inside the player, after source preparation. Unwatched partial titles already chose Resume or Start over in the catalog and skip this prompt.

Review notes
  • Playback stays paused until a choice is made; Resume is the focused default.
  • The prompt is the only place a watched item’s timestamp appears.
  • Play from beginning clears the checkpoint only once the new playback is ready; cancelled or failed preparation keeps it.
  • Different source duration (> max(30 s, 2%)): same layout, text “This source differs in length” with Resume anyway / Start over.
  • Resume still goes through source selection; a checkpoint is not a stream URL.

Review notes
  • Position / duration ≥ 0.90 sets watched, once, and keeps updating the checkpoint. It measures position, not time viewed, so seeking past 90% qualifies.
  • Seeking backward saves the smaller position and never unmarks watched. Auto-marking is one-way.
  • Mark unwatched during a run suppresses auto-marking for the rest of that run, including later seeks; a new playback run may qualify again.
  • Unknown or live duration, loading defaults and failed loads never qualify. EOF is not required.

Review notes
  • Volume is three discrete commands (Mute/Unmute, down, up). No tray slider: not portable.
  • Next / Previous are unsupported by default and omitted here; they stay disabled in OS media controls until a real queue exists.
  • Player actions are disabled (not hidden) with no player. Show Kinema, Transfers, Settings and Quit always work.
  • System volume keys usually change system, not player, volume.
  • Single click may restore Main where supported; the menu always offers Show Kinema.
  • Pause / Stop go through the same commands as the player’s own controls.

08Review checklist

These rules are agreed in the proposal and are shown as settled. Review is limited to styling, layout and copy.

Confirm the wireframes show the agreed rules
  • ☐ Watched titles hide catalog progress bars, percentages and resume timestamps; the checkpoint is retained internally.
  • ☐ Manual unwatched reveals the preserved checkpoint. Clear saved progress is separate from watched and warns during active playback.
  • ☐ The player seek bar is always visible, whatever the watched state.
  • ☐ 90% auto-marks watched, backward seeks save the new position, and manual unwatched suppresses auto-marking for the run.
  • ☐ Closing Main with active playback keeps the separate player visible; closing the player stops it; with no tray, Main is retained.
  • ☐ Next / Previous are unsupported by default; tray volume uses discrete commands.
Styling and copy only
  • ☐ Wording of dialogs, notices and the active-playback warning.
  • ☐ Spacing, emphasis and placement of progress, Resume and watched marks.
