1 · Wide window: compact hero, filters beside the art, list and inspector side by side

The page now uses the same language as Detail. It has artwork fading into the canvas, the poster with a Display title, chips for facts, and the same Segmented control used for seasons. The bordered "control box", the column-header table and the Play modal are gone. The inspector on the right is sticky and owns playback from selection through to launch. Click a row to select it. Press Play to see the inline preparation flow.

What changed and why
  • Hero-lite header. It uses the Detail backdrop recipe (art, directional scrim, fade to canvas), a 104 px poster with the same edge and shadow, an uppercase eyebrow, the type.display title, and the episode as chip + type.heading name. No more 14 px title floating next to a 56 px thumbnail.
  • Filters sit beside the art, under the title, in one unboxed row. Provider and quality use Segmented with counts, the same control as the season switcher, instead of orange-outlined ChoiceChips. Search is the compact SearchField, sort is a MenuButton, and "In my account" is a ToggleChip (shown only with a debrid token). The "remembered" sentence becomes a quiet note above the list.
  • List without a table. No column headers, no per-row surface fill. Rows sit on the canvas and get a surface fill on hover. Selection uses accent.tint plus a 2 px accent left marker (the v3 list-row rule), replacing today's heavy brown flood. Release names use the regular UI face (type.body, medium), not mono, and parsed tags are quiet captions.
  • Inspector replaces drawer + modal. Identity (badges, cleaned name, source), three StatTiles, account presence, and file disclosure scroll inside the panel. The PlayFooter is pinned to the bottom and changes in place: Ready → Preparing → Starting → (closes to Ready) / Error. The Play sheet modal is removed.

2 · Inspector states: the PlayFooter replaces the Play sheet

Everything the modal showed now happens in the footer. The content above stays put, so the user keeps their place. Cancel returns to Ready. Choosing another row while one is preparing shows a one-line banner, and pressing Play on the new row replaces the old session (it is cancelled).

3 · Narrow window (frame < 1040 px): single column + PlayDock

There is no room for a side panel, so the same PlayFooter becomes a floating dock at the bottom of the page (bg.overlay, sheet elevation). The header stacks its filters under the title and they wrap. Still no modal. Clicking a row selects it, and double-click or Enter plays.

5 · Behaviour kept and changed

  • Kept: remembered provider/quality/sort/account-only, per-visit text filter, default first-row selection, explicit pick that survives re-sort, account presence wording ("presence only, not a cache or readiness check"), resume / play-from-beginning, context menu, loading/empty/error/provider-limit states, and Esc/Back returning to Detail with focus restored.
  • Changed: Play no longer opens a modal. Single click selects. Double-click, Enter, or the footer's Play starts preparation in the footer. A row being prepared shows an AccountMark busy "Preparing…" in the list. Starting another stream cancels the previous session. Leaving the page cancels it as well (same ownership and generation rules as today's sheet).
  • Play with: a Segmented between configured services (default: the one whose account contains the release). With one service it becomes a static caption, and with none it shows the "Open Settings" notice in the footer.
  • Breakpoints: ≥ 1040 px frame gets a side inspector (340–400 px), and narrower windows get the PlayDock. Text never shrinks to fit; filters wrap.
